## Logo composition

The master lockup is a three-line stack:

1. **U** — a tall, thin, capital U in teal. It is the hero letter and sits above the wordmark at roughly 1.5× the cap height of the "CALM" row below. The U is the visual anchor of the brand.
2. **CALM** — letterspaced wordmark. The *C* is teal; *ALM* is champagne.
3. **AVIATION** — wider-tracked, smaller, all champagne, below the wordmark.

The U is centred horizontally with the rest of the stack. Never offset it left or right of the wordmark.

## Colour tokens extracted from the logo

- Teal (U + leading C of CALM): `#3BB5C7` — HSL `189 55% 51%`
- Champagne (ALM of CALM + AVIATION): `#BCB17A` — HSL `48 37% 61%`
- Background: pure white `#FFFFFF` or ivory paper `#FBF8EF`

The deeper teal `#2A8A98` in the brand pack is the accent *colour system* for UI and print — not the logo colour. The logo teal is the lighter `#3BB5C7`.

## Logo usage

- **Primary / light backgrounds** — the two-tone lockup (teal + champagne) as shown.
- **Dark backgrounds** — reversed to all-white, or monochrome teal on deep navy. (Use `u-calm-aviation-logo-white.png` when it arrives.)
- **Minimum size** — 72 px tall for the full lockup, 48 px for the U symbol alone.
- **Clear space** — equal to the height of "AVIATION" on all sides.
- **Never** — do not recolour outside teal / champagne / white; do not stretch or skew; do not add drop shadows; do not place the teal logo on saturated teal backgrounds; do not detach the U from the stack for use as a standalone mark outside the approved favicon context.
