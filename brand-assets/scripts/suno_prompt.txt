Solo piano instrumental, 60 BPM, felt piano texture, contemplative and restrained. Sparse single notes with long sustain and slow decay. No percussion, no synth, no bass, no strings, no drums, no vocals. The piece breathes in three movements over 15 seconds: it opens almost imperceptibly with a single held low-register note (the first 4 seconds, calm and quiet, like a morning that has not begun), a gentle two- or three-note middle phrase emerges with slightly more warmth and presence (seconds 4-11, the day unfolding), then dissolves back to near-silence in the final 4 seconds (calm again, the night arrived). Register of Nils Frahm 'Says' or 'Felt', Ólafur Arnalds 'Near Light', Erik Satie 'Gymnopédie No. 1' slowed. Almost imperceptible throughout, present but never demanding attention. No melodic resolution that asks for the listener's focus. The piece accompanies a quiet visual story rather than leading it.
